KNOWN RUNTIME EXPRESSION INFORMATION:

Carlotta.vrm is a VRM0 asset loaded through the installed three-vrm runtime, which remaps VRM0 expression names to VRM1-style runtime names.

Known runtime expressions include:

* `aa`
* `ih`
* `ou`
* `ee`
* `oh`
* `blink`
* `blinkLeft`
* `blinkRight`
* `lookUp`
* `lookDown`
* `lookLeft`
* `lookRight`
* `neutral`
* `happy`
* `angry`
* `sad`
* `relaxed`

There are 17 runtime presets in total according to the previous diagnostic.

The current facial emotion controller uses:

* calm
* happy
* excited
* sad
* angry

and currently maps emotions to a small set of expression weights.

DO NOT assume the semantic name of a preset tells us what it visually does.

For example:

* `angry` may produce little/no visible deformation.
* `sad` may produce little/no visible deformation.
* `happy` may primarily affect the eyelids.
* `relaxed` may potentially affect the mouth.
* phoneme expressions may affect the mouth and MUST NOT be used as emotion expressions.
