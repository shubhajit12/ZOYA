==================================================
EMOTION MODEL
=============

The controller should maintain:

`currentEmotion`

`targetEmotion`

`emotionIntensity`

Use a safe predefined union/type.

Suggested:

type CarlottaEmotion =
| 'calm'
| 'happy'
| 'excited'
| 'sad'
| 'angry';

Do not accept arbitrary strings as bone-control instructions.

Unknown/invalid emotion input must safely resolve to `calm` or otherwise be rejected without affecting bones.

==================================================
PERSISTENCE
===========

Emotion is persistent until explicitly changed.

Example:

setEmotion('happy', 0.7)

Then:

talking
→ listening
→ thinking
→ idle

should preserve the happy emotional baseline.

Changing behavioral state must NOT reset the emotion.

Changing emotion must NOT reset the behavioral state.

Example:

current behavior = talking
current emotion = happy

Calling:

`setEmotion('sad', 0.6)`

should transition the emotional layer toward sad while TALKING continues underneath.

==================================================
SMOOTH EMOTION TRANSITIONS
==========================

Never snap emotion.

When:

`targetEmotion !== currentEmotion`

smoothly blend toward the target.

Use a configurable transition speed.

Target approximately:

0.8–1.5 seconds for normal emotion changes.

Prefer delta-time based exponential smoothing or another stable frame-rate-independent method.

Do NOT use:

* setTimeout
* setInterval
* frame counters
* fixed FPS assumptions

Emotion intensity should also transition smoothly.

For example:

happy 0.2
→
happy 1.0

should gradually increase rather than jump.

Likewise:

happy 1.0
→
calm 0.0

should gradually settle.

==================================================
INTENSITY
=========

`emotionIntensity` must be safely clamped:

`0.0 → 1.0`

Use intensity to scale the emotion offsets.

At intensity 0:

emotion layer should contribute essentially no motion.

At intensity 0.5:

subtle emotion.

At intensity 1:

strongest allowed version.

Even at intensity 1, movement must remain restrained and believable.

Do NOT create exaggerated anime-like movement.

==================================================
IMPORTANT ROTATION RULE
=======================

NEVER modify a bone using its previous animated rotation.

Never do:

`bone.rotation.x += ...`

Every frame must derive the final result from captured base quaternions plus procedural offsets.

The existing controller already follows this principle.

Preserve it.

Do not introduce quaternion drift.

==================================================
CALM
====

Calm should be the neutral baseline.

Characteristics:

* relaxed shoulders
* neutral head
* subtle breathing-compatible posture
* very small natural drift

Do not duplicate the existing idle breathing.

Calm's contribution should be close to neutral.

Think:

"emotionally relaxed"

not:

"another idle animation."

==================================================
HAPPY
=====

Happy should add subtle positive energy.

Use small:

* chest openness
* slight head lift
* slight shoulder openness
* gentle body sway

Suggested maximum contribution at intensity 1:

head:
~0.5–1.5 degrees

neck:
~0.3–1 degree

shoulders:
~0.3–1 degree

chest/spine:
~0.3–1 degree

Keep everything smooth.

Happy must work naturally with talking.

==================================================
EXCITED
=======

Excited is stronger than happy.

Use:

* slightly more energetic chest
* slightly more active shoulders
* slightly greater head movement
* slightly faster natural variation

Still:

NO shaking.

NO constant bobbing.

NO exaggerated movement.

Suggested maximum contribution:

head:
~1–2 degrees

neck:
~0.5–1.2 degrees

shoulders:
~0.5–1.5 degrees

chest/spine:
~0.5–1.2 degrees

These are upper bounds, not targets.

==================================================
SAD
===

Sad should reduce energy.

Use:

* slight head lowering
* subtle shoulder drop
* slightly reduced posture openness
* slower emotional movement

Keep the effect subtle.

Do NOT rotate the entire character downward.

Do NOT interfere with the relaxed pose foundation.

Suggested maximum contribution:

head:
~0.5–1.5 degrees downward

neck:
~0.3–1 degree

shoulders:
~0.3–1 degree

chest/spine:
~0.3–1 degree

==================================================
ANGRY
=====

Angry should communicate restrained tension.

Use:

* slight forward head posture
* subtle shoulder tension
* small controlled head/neck movement
* slightly stronger chest posture

Do NOT create aggressive shaking.

Do NOT make large rotations.

Do NOT modify facial expressions.

The eventual expression system will handle facial anger.

Keep body language believable.

==================================================

============

IMPORTANT:

Do not implement emotions as obvious looping animations.

Keep amplitudes small.

Use deterministic variation.

Randomness is NOT required.

If any variation is used, it must be deterministic/lightweight and must not cause emotion instability.

Emotion should feel like a persistent posture/energy bias with subtle movement.
