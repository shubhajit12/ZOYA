# POSE DESIGN

The goal is NOT to make a perfect animation pose.

The goal is simply to turn the hard T-pose into a believable neutral standing pose.

## Shoulders

Bring both shoulders subtly downward/inward from the horizontal T-pose.

Keep the movement conservative.

Do not rotate them dramatically.

## Upper Arms

Move:

```text
leftUpperArm
rightUpperArm
```

from the horizontal T-pose toward a natural resting position beside the torso.

Use mirrored left/right values.

Do not exaggerate.

## Lower Arms / Elbows

Give both elbows a small natural bend.

The arms should not remain perfectly straight.

Keep the bend subtle and symmetrical.

## Hands

Initially leave:

```text
leftHand
rightHand
```

at their existing rest orientation unless inspection proves a small correction is necessary.

Do not manipulate fingers.

## Spine / Chest

Do NOT change spine/chest in the first implementation unless absolutely required to prevent visible deformation.

The procedural idle system will handle tiny posture/breathing offsets later.

## Neck / Head

Do NOT change neck/head.

Preserve their existing rest orientation.

## Hips / Legs

DO NOT modify:

```text
hips
leftUpperLeg
rightUpperLeg
leftLowerLeg
rightLowerLeg
leftFoot
rightFoot
leftToes
rightToes
```

No IK.

No foot placement.

No locomotion.

## Eyes

DO NOT modify:

```text
leftEye
rightEye
```

Eye tracking owns those.

## Physics / Secondary Bones

DO NOT modify any:

* hair bones
* skirt bones
* accessory bones
* spring bones
* unmapped bones
* `!` helper/physics bones
