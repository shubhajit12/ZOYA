ROOT CAUSE:
Carlotta.vrm contains VRM0 phoneme presets:

* A → a
* I → i
* U → u
* E → e
* O → o

The installed three-vrm runtime remaps these VRM0 presets to VRM1 runtime names:

* a → aa
* i → ih
* u → ou
* e → ee
* o → oh

Therefore carlottaLipSync.ts currently looking up `getExpression('a')` is wrong.
