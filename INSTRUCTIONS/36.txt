==============

Cache the analyser and reusable buffers.

==================================================
MOUTH MOVEMENT
==============

The mouth should respond to actual speech energy.

Requirements:

* mouth closed during silence
* mouth opens during speech
* stronger audio → generally larger mouth opening
* quieter audio → smaller opening
* smooth attack
* smooth release
* no rapid jitter
* no constant fully-open mouth
* no obvious mechanical pulsing

Use smoothing.

Do NOT directly map raw instantaneous amplitude to expression weight.

For example:

smoothedAmplitude → mouthWeight

with separate attack/release smoothing if appropriate.

Keep expression weights clamped to:

`0.0 → 1.0`

but normally stay below maximum.

==================================================
ANTI-JITTER
===========

Raw audio amplitude can be noisy.

Do NOT make the mouth follow every tiny waveform fluctuation.

Use lightweight smoothing.

A reasonable starting point:

attack:
~0.08–0.15 seconds

release:
~0.12–0.25 seconds

These are starting ranges only.

Tune after inspecting the existing implementation.

The result should look like natural speaking rather than:

OPEN
CLOSE
OPEN
CLOSE

every few milliseconds.

==================================================
SILENCE DETECTION
=================

There should be a small effective speech threshold.

Very quiet background/noise should not constantly open the mouth.

When audio falls below the effective threshold:

gradually return mouth weight toward zero.

Do not snap shut.

==================================================
EXPRESSION DESIGN
=================

Do NOT attempt full phoneme recognition unless the project already has the necessary infrastructure.

This pass is about reliable natural speech motion.

If multiple mouth expressions already exist and the existing pipeline supports them safely, you may use them.

Do not invent an unnecessarily complex viseme system.
